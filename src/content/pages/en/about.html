Who we are

A story of passion and expertise

Studio 3 was born from a passion for hairdressing and doing things right. After more than 14 years of experience in the profession, the idea was simple: create a salon in our image, where quality of gesture, attention to each person and the pleasure of taking care of hair are prioritized.

Located in the heart of Esch-sur-Alzette, Studio 3 was designed as a place where you can take your time. A salon where every appointment is a moment for yourself, guided by listening, advice and respect for your style.

What guides us

Our values

01
Listening

Each person is different. Before we start, we take the time to understand your desires, your style and your lifestyle. This listening allows us to create cuts, balayage and colors that truly resemble you.

02
Expertise

Hairdressing is a precise craft. We continue to train in current techniques to offer you natural, elegant and long-lasting results. Every detail counts, from diagnosis to finishing touches.

03
Natural beauty

At Studio 3, we don't try to impose a trend. Our role is to reveal what enhances you, respecting your personality and the nature of your hair.

04
Quality products

We select recognized professional products for their effectiveness and respect for hair. Treatments designed for hair health and lasting results.

05
Creativity

Every cut is unique. Our job is to adapt techniques and shapes to create a harmonious, modern result tailored to you.

06
Warm welcome

Studio 3 is a place where you feel good. A quiet salon in the heart of Esch-sur-Alzette where we take the time to welcome, advise and support you.
